# Unicorn Birthday Treasure Hunt — Final Story

## Purpose

This file defines the **final story and narrative flow** for the birthday treasure-hunt web app.

The app is for children aged **5–7** and should feel like **one continuous magical birthday adventure**.

The children cannot read much yet, so all child-facing scenes should be:

- image-led
- visually clear
- very short
- easy to understand from a TV/iPad
- supported by simple lightweight animation

The outdoor app screens are mainly for the **organizer/parents**. Children only need to see the magical reward moments, destination reveal, piñata mission, and finale.

---

# 1. Story premise

Today is a very special day in the magical forest.

The little unicorn is turning **6 years old — just like Lucy**.

The unicorn is happy and wants to celebrate this beautiful day with its friends.

Its birthday is made magical by three special powers:

- ⭐ **Mut**
- 🌈 **Zusammenhalt**
- 💎 **Klugheit**

But suddenly, a magical storm arrives.

The storm blows the three powers away.

Then the unicorn disappears too.

The children become three rescue teams:

- 👾 **Die kleinen Monster**
- 🐙 **Die kleinen Oktopusse**
- 🐊 **Die kleinen Krokodile**

Their mission is to recover the three powers, follow the unicorn's clues, find the hidden treasure, and bring the magic back so the birthday celebration can continue.

# 4. Outdoor gameplay principle

The outdoor section is mainly a **parent/organizer interface**.

Parents should see:

- current level
- short rules
- team status
- completion controls
- collected magic symbols

Children do not need to read the physical game instructions from the app.

When a team completes a task, however, the organizer should briefly show the children a short reward animation.

---

# 5. Level 1 — Rainbow Path with 5 mats

## Story meaning

The first magical power is:

# ⭐ MUT

The children complete the physical Rainbow Path using **5 mats**.

When a team finishes, show a short reward screen.

Example:

**„Die kleinen Monster haben MUT gefunden!“**

Use the appropriate team name.

Animate ⭐ into that team's collection.

When all three teams have completed Level 1:

# **MUT IST ZURÜCK!**

Short supporting text:

**„Die erste Zauberkraft habt ihr gefunden.“**

# 7. Level 3 — Letter treasure hunt

Begin with a short transition.

Text:

**„Das Einhorn hat eine geheime Spur hinterlassen …“**

Do **not** show the target words to the children before they search.

The three teams physically find:

- 👾 Little Monsters → **BREIT**
- 🐙 Little Octopuses → **WIESEN**
- 🐊 Little Crocodiles → **SCHULE**

# BREIT

# WIESEN

# SCHULE

**„Was könnte das bedeuten?“**

# **ZAUBERWÖRTER VERBINDEN**

**BREIT + WIESEN + SCHULE**

→

# **BREITWIESENSCHULE**

Pause briefly.

Then reveal:

# **HOF**

The third magical power is awarded:

# 💎 KLUGHEIT

On the existing **„Der nächste Ort: Breitwiesenschule Hof“** page, show the button:

# **ZUM SCHULHOF-SCHATZSUCHE STARTEN**

# 12. Final birthday screen

This is the most beautiful and celebratory screen in the app.

Use the supplied PNG birthday/unicorn image as the main visual asset.

Do not redraw or recreate that image programmatically.

The screen should be suitable for:

- iPad
- laptop
- TV projection
- a final birthday memory/photo moment

Large text:

# **Alles Gute zum 6. Geburtstag, Lucy!**

Also dynamically show the names of all participating children on the side.

# 13. Visual and implementation rules

## Child-facing scenes

Use:

- one strong illustration or scene
- very little text
- large typography
- simple visual storytelling
- lightweight animation

Do not rely on children reading long instructions.

## Outdoor parent screens

Prioritize:

- clarity
- fast controls
- team progress
- easy completion/undo
- collected symbols

## Story consistency

The following elements should remain visually consistent throughout:

- the same unicorn
- the same three team mascots
- the same three magical powers
- the same magical storm visual language
- the same birthday atmosphere
- the same illustration style

## Technical rule

Keep story copy and scene definitions separate from application logic.

Preferred:

```text
content.js   -> story text, scene content, team/reward labels
app.js       -> state, navigation, game logic
styles.css   -> layout and animations
```

The wording in this file should be easy to update later without changing the app logic.

Do not add extra lore, game mechanics, complex animation systems, or additional story branches.
